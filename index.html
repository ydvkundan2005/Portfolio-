03 / PROJECTS

Selected work.

A selection of practical applications spanning desktop connectivity, AI-assisted learning, and location-based coordination.

DESKTOP / WIRELESS CAMERA↗

FloatCam

Transforms a smartphone into a wireless webcam, streaming live video to a floating desktop preview on macOS and Windows over a local Wi-Fi network. Users can connect through a QR code and adjust the preview's position, zoom, and opacity.

CROSS-PLATFORM CONNECTIVITY↗

LocalBridge

Connects Android devices and macOS computers over a local network, making it easy to discover and pair devices, transfer files, and share clipboard content through native interfaces.

AI-ASSISTED LEARNING✦

CampusGPT

A multimodal AI learning platform that helps students interact with educational content and access AI-assisted learning features through one integrated application, including AI-powered responses to support the learning experience.

BLOOD-DONOR COORDINATION↗

BloodByte

A browser-based blood-donor coordination prototype where users can create accounts, share location-aware blood requests, discover nearby requests, and coordinate through real-time one-to-one messaging. This is a learning project, not a production medical or emergency service.
